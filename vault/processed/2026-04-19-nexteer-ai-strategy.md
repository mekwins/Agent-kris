# Nexteer AI Strategy

Nexteer Automotive's AI strategy is led by Kris Mekwinski (Director of Global Enterprise Architecture) under the AI Center of Excellence. The strategy spans five pillars and targets both cost reduction and top-line growth.

## Five Pillars
1. **Access Levels Ladder (L1–L4)** — tiered AI access model for employees
2. **Vibe Coding Training** — structured program for AI-assisted development
3. **Platform & Infrastructure** — Coolify-based governed PaaS; Claude Enterprise procurement
4. **Small Language Models** — targeted SLM deployment for domain-specific use cases
5. **Frontier AI Team Charter** — formal team structure for advanced AI R&D

## Strategic Framing
- Dual-layer model: **Operational AI** (cost/efficiency) + **Cognitive AI** (engineering velocity, RFQ speed, capital allocation, legal automation)
- Three horizons of human-AI collaboration
- AI Innovators Champions network across regions/functions
- 140+ use cases tracked on monday.com board across 8+ functions

## Key Presentations Built
- C-suite AI Frontier Strategy deck (Andy Wilson audience)
- AI Innovators evolution framework
- Vibe Coding governance framework
- 90-day roadmap April–June 2026 with 6 leadership decisions needed
- Finance × IT AI portfolio presentation
- monday.com Global Project Ledger (324 projects, live MCP board data)

## Governance Framework
- Green/yellow/red risk zones for vibe coding
- Shadow IT controls
- Approval process for AI-generated code deployment
- Coolify team isolation (Vibecoders team), API token scoping

## Top-Line Growth Focus
- RFQ response speed
- Capital allocation optimization
- Engineering velocity via vibe coding
- Legal contract analysis automation (CLM — Jonas Regenfuss / Diane McKellar)
