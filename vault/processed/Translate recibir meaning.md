In **Spanish**, the verb **“decir”** means **“to say”** or **“to tell.”**

---

### 🔑 Key details:

- **Infinitive:** *decir*
- **Irregular verb**: it changes in several forms.
- **First person singular (yo):** *digo*
- **Stem change (e → i):** e.g., *tú dices*, *ellos dicen*

---

### 📝 Common uses:

- *Decir la verdad* → “to tell the truth”
- *Decir una mentira* → “to tell a lie”
- *¿Qué dijiste?* → “What did you say?”
- *Me dijo que venía* → “He/She told me (that) he/she was coming”

---

👉 Pro tip: When you mean “to tell *someone*,” you usually use an indirect object pronoun:

- *Me dijo* → “He/She told **me** ”
- *Te digo* → “I tell **you** ”
- *Les dije* → “I told **them** ”
