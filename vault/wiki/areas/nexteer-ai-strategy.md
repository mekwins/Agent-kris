# Nexteer AI Strategy

Nexteer Automotive's AI strategy is led by [[Kris Mekwinski]] (Director of Global Enterprise Architecture) under the AI Center of Excellence. The strategy spans five pillars and targets both cost reduction and top-line growth across global manufacturing, engineering, finance, quality, and supply chain functions.

## Five Pillars
1. **Access Levels Ladder (L1–L4)** — tiered AI access model for employees, from consumer tools to frontier R&D
2. **Vibe Coding Training** — structured program for AI-assisted development; governed via [[Coolify Infrastructure at Nexteer]]
3. **Platform & Infrastructure** — Coolify-based governed PaaS; Claude Enterprise procurement (~50–100 users)
4. **Small Language Models** — targeted SLM deployment for domain-specific use cases
5. **Frontier AI Team Charter** — formal team structure for advanced AI R&D

## Strategic Framing
- **Dual-layer model**: Operational AI (cost/efficiency) + Cognitive AI (engineering velocity, RFQ speed, capital allocation, legal automation)
- Three horizons of human-AI collaboration
- AI Innovators Champions network across regions and functions
- 140+ use cases tracked on monday.com board across 8+ functions

## Governance Framework
- Green/yellow/red risk zones for vibe coding deployments
- Shadow IT controls — Coolify provides a sanctioned outlet
- Approval process for AI-generated code deployment
- Coolify team isolation (Vibecoders team) with API token scoping
