- CEFR is the international standard for language proficiency (A1 → C2)

## Spanish Verbs Covered

### recibir — to receive
- Regular -ir verb

### poner — to put / to place
- **Irregular** — yo form: *pongo*
- Common uses: *poner la mesa* (set the table), *poner la televisión* (turn on the TV), *poner atención* (pay attention)
- Reflexive use: *ponerse triste* = to become sad

### decir — to say / to tell
- **Highly irregular** — stems change: *dic-* (present), *dij-* (preterite), *dir-* (future)
- yo form: *digo*

#### decir — Mini Conjugation Chart

| Tense | Yo | Tú | Él/Ella | Nosotros | Vosotros | Ellos |
|-------|----|----|---------|----------|----------|-------|
| Present | digo | dices | dice | decimos | decís | dicen |
| Preterite | dije | dijiste | dijo | dijimos | dijisteis | dijeron |
| Future | diré | dirás | dirá | diremos | diréis | dirán |

## CEFR Language Levels Reference

| Level | Name | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| A1 | Beginner | Basic everyday phrases, simple introductions |
| A2 | Elementary | Routine tasks, simple direct exchanges |
| B1 | Intermediate | Most everyday situations, simple connected text |
| B2 | Upper Intermediate | Fluent interaction with native speakers, complex texts |
| C1 | Advanced | Flexible, fluent use for academic/professional purposes |
| C2 | Proficient | Near-native, effortless comprehension and expression |
