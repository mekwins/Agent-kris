# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; formerly Director of AI at Tesla and founding member of OpenAI. Known for making deep learning accessible through lectures, blog posts, and viral threads. Active on X as @karpathy.

## Key Ideas & Quotes

- **LLM as Knowledge Compiler** — "A large fraction of my recent token throughput is going less into manipulating code, and more into manipulating knowledge." (April 2026)
- Advocates for treating LLMs as compilers that transform raw source documents into structured, interlinked Markdown wikis rather than using RAG at small–medium scale.
- "I think there is room here for an incredible new product instead of a hacky collection of scripts." — on the LLM knowledge base pattern.
- Coined the term **vibe coding** (coding by feel/intent with LLM assistance) and has extended it to **vibe research**.

## Notable Contributions

- April 2026 viral thread on [[LLM Knowledge Base Pattern]] — described a full workflow: ingest → compile → view (Obsidian) → ask → create → maintain.
- Published GitHub Gist as a replicable "idea file" for the pattern: https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f
- Proposed future direction: use personal knowledge bases to generate synthetic training data and fine-tune an LLM so it "knows" the KB in its weights.
