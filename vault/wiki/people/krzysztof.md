Spanish language learner tracked in the vault. As of April 2026 he is at a solid **A2 level** (approaching A2+), having completed Units 3–6 of the Cuaderno de Tareas course.

- Grammar accuracy is good on regular patterns; errors surface on ser/estar distinction, gustar pronoun agreement, and irregular imperative forms

## Strengths
- Reflexive verb conjugation: accurate across most persons
- Time prepositions (desde/hasta/por/de): well applied
- Restaurant and food vocabulary: strong
- Imperative formation: regular verbs solid
- Gustar structure: understands gusta vs gustan distinction
- Free writing (text compositions): shows real communicative fluency at A2

## Error Patterns to Watch
- SER vs ESTAR confusion (location always needs estar)
- Reflexive vs non-reflexive choice (*lavar* vs *lavarse*)
- Gustar pronoun order and agreement with plural subjects
- Irregular imperative tú forms (pon, haz, ven, ve — not yet drilled)
- Ordinal number endings (primero/primera agreement)

## Recommended Next Steps
1. Drill ser/estar distinction with error-correction exercises (biggest gap)
2. Memorise the 8 irregular tú imperatives: pon, haz, ven, ve, sé, ten, sal, di
3. Practice gustar agreement with plural nouns and 3rd-person subjects
4. Write 1 short paragraph per week using free-writing prompts
5. Target: A2+ / pre-B1 by end of Units 8–9
