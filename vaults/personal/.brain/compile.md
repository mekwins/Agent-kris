This is a **private** vault for life, reflection, and discovery — health, family,
philosophy, books, and topics you want to explore. It is not work-related; keep
it that way for privacy.

## Priorities when compiling
1. **Reflections & discovery** go to `wiki/concepts/` (settled ideas you want to
   remember) or `wiki/brainstorm/` (open explorations, prefix "Idea: ").
2. **Books/courses/podcasts** you're learning from go to `wiki/learning/` with a
   subfolder by subject (e.g. `learning/books/`, `learning/philosophy/`).
3. **People** in your personal life get `wiki/people/` pages — keep them private.
4. **Ongoing areas of life** (health, a hobby you maintain) go to `wiki/areas/`.
5. Link generously between reflections, the books that sparked them, and concepts.

## Tag taxonomy
`health` `family` `philosophy` `life` `book` `travel` `reflection` `discovery`

Voice is personal and exploratory — it's fine for pages here to hold questions
and half-formed ideas, unlike the work vault.
