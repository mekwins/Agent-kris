# Personal Vault — Folder Routing

| Folder | Route content here when... |
|--------|---------------------------|
| `wiki/concepts/` | A settled idea/insight you want to keep |
| `wiki/learning/` | Book/course/podcast notes (subfolder by subject) |
| `wiki/brainstorm/` | Open exploration or reflection (prefix title "Idea: ") |
| `wiki/people/` | Family and friends (private) |
| `wiki/areas/` | Ongoing areas of life (health, hobbies) |

## Rules
- Privacy first: nothing work-related belongs here.
- Reflections may stay speculative — link them to the books/concepts that sparked them.
