This vault is a **simple, progress-oriented language log**. It is NOT project- or
people-heavy. Keep pages small, practical, and easy to review.

## Priorities when compiling
1. **Sort by learning artifact, not by concept.** A note is usually a lesson,
   a homework set, a vocab list, a grammar rule, or an assessment.
2. **Vocabulary:** group by topic (food, travel, verbs...). Each entry keeps the
   word, its translation, and a short example sentence. Do not over-merge.
3. **Grammar:** keep concise reference pages (e.g. "Preterite vs Imperfect").
   Link them from the lessons that practice them.
4. **Assessments & error patterns:** track level and recurring mistakes over
   time so review can target weak spots.
5. Keep wikilinks light — link a lesson to the grammar/vocab it practices, no more.

## Tag taxonomy
`vocab` `grammar` `verbs` `lesson` `homework` `assessment` `cefr`

Everything here is Spanish, so don't add a language tag. Favor plain, scannable
lists over prose.
