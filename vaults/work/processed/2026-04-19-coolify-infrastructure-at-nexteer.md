# Coolify Infrastructure at Nexteer

Coolify is the governed PaaS sandbox deployed by Kris Mekwinski for Nexteer's Vibe Coding initiative. It provides a controlled environment for employee-built AI applications.

## Setup Details
- **URL**: 192.168.50.60:8000
- **Team isolation**: Vibecoders team
- **API token scoping**: per-team/per-project isolation
- **Deployed apps**: FiveWhyAI (confirmed)

## Purpose
- Governed deployment environment for vibe coders
- Prevents shadow IT by giving a sanctioned outlet
- Enables monitoring, access control, and audit trails
- Part of the green/yellow/red risk zone governance model
